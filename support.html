How can we help?

Answers to common questions. Still stuck? Get in touch.

What do I need?

A Mac running Turnhall Home 1.1.0 or later, on Apple silicon, plus an iPad or iPhone with the Turnhall app (iOS or iPadOS 27). The Home does the work; the apps are your window onto it.

How do I pair a device?

Open Turnhall Home on your Mac and show the pairing QR code. In the Turnhall app on your iPad or iPhone, tap Scan pairing code and point the camera at it. The camera is used only for this.

Can I use it away from home?

Yes. Once paired, you can follow, answer and steer your tasks from anywhere, as long as your Mac is on and running Turnhall Home. Terminal and file transfer are the exception: they work on your local network only.

Why can't I see Terminal or File transfer?

Both are off until you allow them for each device. In Turnhall Home, open Devices, choose the device and switch on Terminal or File transfer.

Your device must also be on the same network as the Mac. The About screen shows the current status.

Which AI engines and accounts?

Claude, Cursor, Codex and Antigravity, each task on its own engine. Turnhall uses your own accounts and keys, set up in Turnhall Home, and sends requests to those providers under their terms. We don't hold any accounts for you.

Where are my files?

Always on the Mac that runs Turnhall Home. The apps on your iPad and iPhone show and edit them over your connection; they don't keep a separate copy of your projects.

Something isn't working
  1. Update Turnhall Home and the Turnhall app to the latest versions.
  2. Restart Turnhall Home on your Mac.
  3. Pair the device again with a fresh QR code.
  4. Still stuck? Send feedback through TestFlight, or write to us below.
