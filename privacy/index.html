Privacy

Effective date: 20 November 2026

Turnhall and Turnhall Home don't collect, store or share any personal data with us or with third parties. There are no accounts, analytics, tracking or ads.

The iPhone and iPad app connects only to the Turnhall Home you pair it with, which runs on your own Mac. Everything you see in the app (tasks, messages, files) stays between your devices and your Mac. When you use AI engines (for example Claude, Cursor, Codex or Gemini), Turnhall Home sends your requests to those providers using your own accounts and keys, under their terms and privacy policies.

Camera access is used only to scan the pairing QR code. The microphone (if you use dictation) is used only while you dictate.
